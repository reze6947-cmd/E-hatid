# E-Hatid — Off-page SEO / Link Building

On-page is done; off-page is where most ranking gains will come from while the app
has few backlinks. This is a priority-ordered plan. Start from the top.

## 1. Google Business Profile (highest ROI, free)

- Create a Google Business Profile for "E-Hatid" as a food delivery service.
- Verify with the app domain; add the site URL, service area (start with launch
  city/provinces), photos of the app and stall screenshots.
- Post weekly updates (new stalls, rider recruitment) — posts get indexed quickly.
- Encourage stall partners and customers to leave Google reviews.

## 2. Local directory listings

Add the business to these (each is a dofollow backlink + local citation):
- Google Maps (via Business Profile above)
- Facebook & Instagram page (link in bio → app store / guest home)
- LinkedIn company page
- Startup/SaaS directories: Product Hunt (launch post), AlternativeTo, BetaList
- Filipino-focused: PH Startup communities, Reddit r/philippines (soft mentions only)

## 3. Partnership & digital PR

- **Stall partners:** add a "Powered by E-Hatid" badge/logo on partner stall social
  pages and their Facebook pages. Ask stall owners to link to their stall page
  (`/stall/<id>/menu`).
- **Rider recruitment:** post "we're hiring riders in <city>" in job boards and
  community FB groups — links back to `/apply/rider`.
- **University/barangay events:** sponsor small local food events; event pages link back.

## 4. Content-driven links (do after `CONTENT_PLAN.md`)

- Publish 1–2 genuinely useful pages (e.g. "How much does food delivery cost in the
  Philippines?" style guides) and pitch them to PH food/finance blogs for citation.
- Reach out to food vloggers/FoodToker accounts for honest reviews of the delivery
  experience — YouTube/FB/TikTok backlinks and brand searches.

## 5. Internal linking (on-page, free)

- Stall pages must link to the vendor's public page and "Browse more stalls" → Guest Home.
- FAQ answers should (when relevant) link to `/apply/vendor`, `/apply/rider`, `/guest/home`.

## Monitoring

- Submit `https://ehatid.vercel.app/sitemap.xml` in Google Search Console (GSC)
  once live, and Bing Webmaster Tools.
- Track referring domains monthly in GSC → Links. Goal: 10+ quality referring domains
  in the first 90 days post-launch.
